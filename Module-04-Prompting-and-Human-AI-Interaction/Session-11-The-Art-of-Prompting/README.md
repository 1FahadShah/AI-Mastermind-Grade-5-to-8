# Session 11 - The Art of Prompting

> **Track A (Grades 5-8)** | **Module 4** | **Session 11 of 25**

---

## Overview

Session 11 introduces prompting as a skill for communicating effectively with
AI systems.

The session begins with the difference between vague and clear instructions
and then introduces the basic ideas behind writing better prompts.

It prepares learners to move from basic prompting into more advanced
prompting techniques in Session 12.

---

## Purpose

The session is designed to help learners:

- Understand what a prompt is
- Understand why clear instructions matter
- Recognize the difference between vague and specific prompts
- Improve prompts by adding useful information
- Practice writing prompts for different tasks
- Understand prompting as a skill when working with AI

## Session Construction

The session progresses through the following sequence:

**Connect → Explain → Compare → Demonstrate → Practice → Improve → Reflect → Extend**

### Connect

The session begins with a familiar communication example where a vague
message can lead to a confused or unhelpful response.

This establishes the connection between the quality of an instruction and the
response it produces.

### Explain

A prompt is introduced as an instruction or request given to an AI system.

Examples include asking AI to write a birthday poem, explain photosynthesis,
or suggest science-project ideas.

### Compare

Learners compare vague prompts with clearer and more specific prompts and
observe how additional information can change the resulting response.

### Demonstrate

The session demonstrates how prompts can be improved by providing the AI with
more useful direction and context.

### Practice

Learners write and test prompts for different tasks and observe the responses
they produce.

### Improve

Learners revise their prompts based on the results and consider what
additional information could make the instruction clearer.

### Reflect

Learners consider why prompting matters and how their instructions affect the
AI's response.

### Extend

The session prepares learners for Session 12, which introduces more advanced
prompting techniques.

## Examples & Explanations

The session uses everyday communication to introduce the idea that unclear
instructions can lead to unclear responses.

Examples include prompts for:

- Creative writing
- Explaining concepts
- Generating ideas

The session emphasizes that better prompts can help produce better AI
responses.

---

## Hands-On Component

Learners write, test, and improve prompts for different tasks.

The activity gives learners direct experience with how changes to an
instruction can affect an AI-generated response.

---

## Reflection

Learners reflect on:

- What changed when they improved a prompt
- Which instructions produced more useful responses
- What information was useful to include
- Why prompting is a skill worth developing

---

## Assessment & Extension

The session includes examples, prompt comparisons, hands-on prompt writing,
testing, discussion, and reflection.

The session prepares learners to apply more advanced prompting techniques in
Session 12.
