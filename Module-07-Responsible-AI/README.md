# Module 07 - Responsible AI

> **Track A (Grades 5-8)** | **Module 7 of 8** | **2 Hours** | **Sessions 20-21**

---

## Overview

Module 07 introduces learners to responsible AI through the topics of bias, fairness, safety, misuse, and governance.

The module moves from examining how AI systems can produce different outcomes for different groups to considering how AI can be used safely and responsibly. Learners explore real-world cases, analyze potential risks, and consider the role of safeguards, human oversight, rules, and governance.

---

## Module Purpose

The module is designed to help learners:

- Understand bias and fairness in AI
- Identify possible sources of AI bias
- Explore real-world examples of biased AI systems
- Understand potential risks and misuse of AI
- Consider how AI systems can be used safely
- Explore the purpose of safeguards
- Understand the basic idea of AI governance
- Recognize the role of humans in responsible AI use
- Think critically about responsible decisions involving AI

## Session Breakdown

### Session 20 - Bias & Fairness

Introduces bias and fairness in AI.

Learners explore how AI systems can work differently for different groups and examine possible sources of bias, including:

- Data
- Algorithms
- Human decisions

The session uses real-world cases including Gender Shades (2018), Amazon hiring AI (2018), and COMPAS (2016).

The hands-on **Junior AI Ethicist** activity gives learners an opportunity to conduct a simplified AI audit, identify potential sources of bias, consider fairness, and suggest improvements.

---

### Session 21 - AI Safety, Misuse & Governance

Explores what can happen when AI systems are used irresponsibly, intentionally misused, or deployed without appropriate safeguards.

Learners examine potential risks including:

- Misleading or harmful content
- Misuse of AI-generated media
- Privacy concerns
- Overreliance on AI
- Unsafe applications of AI

The session introduces safeguards, human oversight, rules, policies, and AI governance as ways of guiding responsible AI use.

The hands-on scenario activity asks learners to identify risks, affected groups, safeguards, human involvement, and possible rules or policies.

---

## Design Approach

The module uses a progression from **fairness → safety → responsible use**.

Session 20 focuses on how AI systems can produce different outcomes and how bias can be identified and addressed.

Session 21 expands the discussion to broader AI risks, including misuse, safeguards, human oversight, and governance.

Both sessions use real-world cases or scenarios to move the discussion beyond definitions and into practical analysis.

## Assessment & Reflection

Assessment and reflection across the module include:

- Bias identification
- Real-world case analysis
- AI auditing
- Fairness analysis
- Risk identification
- Misuse scenarios
- Safeguard analysis
- Governance discussions
- Human oversight
- Discussion and reflection
- Session-level knowledge checks

Learners are encouraged to consider not only what AI can do, but also what responsibilities come with developing and using AI systems.

---

## Module Connection

Module 07 introduces the responsible use of AI after learners have explored AI foundations, machine learning, generative AI, prompting, AI domains, and real-world applications.

The module concludes by giving learners a set of responsible AI considerations that can inform the research and project work in the final capstone module.
