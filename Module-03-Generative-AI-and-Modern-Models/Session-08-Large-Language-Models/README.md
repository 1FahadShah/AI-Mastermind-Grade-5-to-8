# Session 08 - Large Language Models

> **Track A (Grades 5-8)** | **Module 3** | **Session 8 of 25**

---

## Overview

Session 08 introduces Large Language Models (LLMs) and how they work with
language.

The session focuses on the idea that an LLM generates language by predicting
what comes next based on patterns learned from its training data.

It also introduces the capabilities and limitations learners should
understand when working with language models.

---

## Purpose

The session is designed to help learners:

- Understand what a Large Language Model is
- Understand the basic idea of next-word prediction
- Recognize what LLMs can do with language
- Recognize common limitations of LLMs
- Understand why LLM responses can be incorrect
- Distinguish language generation from human understanding
- Develop the habit of checking important AI-generated information

## Session Construction

The session progresses through the following sequence:

**Recap → Introduce → Explain → Explore → Compare → Identify Limitations → Reflect → Extend**

### Recap

The session connects back to Generative AI from Session 07 and the idea of
AI creating new content.

### Introduce

Large Language Models are introduced as models designed to work with
language.

### Explain

The session uses the idea of predicting the next word to provide a simple
explanation of how an LLM generates text.

The model uses patterns learned from its training data to produce a response.

### Explore

Learners examine common things LLMs can do, including:

- Answering questions
- Writing stories and poems
- Summarizing information
- Translating
- Explaining concepts
- Brainstorming
- Helping with code

### Compare

The session compares a human brain with a language model.

The comparison highlights differences in patterns, meaning, feelings,
memories, and awareness.

### Identify Limitations

Learners are introduced to limitations including:

- Hallucinations
- Outdated information
- Training-data bias
- Context limitations
- Lack of true understanding
- Lack of feelings

### Reflect

Learners consider the difference between producing a convincing response
and actually understanding the subject.

The session emphasizes checking important information rather than assuming
that an AI response is always correct.

### Extend

The session prepares learners for Session 09, which focuses specifically on
AI hallucinations and why AI can confidently produce incorrect information.

## Examples & Explanations

The session uses the next-word prediction idea as a simple way to explain
how language models generate text.

A comparison between the human brain and a language model is used to discuss
differences between human understanding and pattern-based language
generation.

---

## Hands-On Component

The session focuses primarily on exploring and discussing how language models
work and what they can do.

Learners examine examples of LLM capabilities and limitations and consider
whether generating a response means that the model actually understands what
it is saying.

---

## Reflection

Learners reflect on:

- How an LLM generates language
- What patterns have to do with its responses
- Whether an LLM actually understands what it writes
- Why an AI response can sound convincing while still being wrong
- Why important information should be checked

---

## Assessment & Extension

The session includes concept exploration, comparison activities, discussion,
and reflection around LLM capabilities and limitations.

Learners are encouraged to apply the principle of checking important
information when working with AI-generated responses.
