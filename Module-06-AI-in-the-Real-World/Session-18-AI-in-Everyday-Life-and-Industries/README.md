# Session 18 - AI in Everyday Life & Industries

> **Track A (Grades 5-8)** | **Module 6** | **Session 18 of 25**

---

## Overview

Session 18 explores how AI is used in everyday life and across different industries.

The session moves from individual AI applications to broader real-world use cases, helping learners recognize how AI can support people in areas such as healthcare, finance, transportation, entertainment, and education.

---

## Purpose

The session is designed to help learners:

- Identify AI applications in everyday life
- Explore how AI is used across different industries
- Understand how AI can support people in different roles
- Apply the Sense → Think → Act model to real-world AI applications
- Recognize the role of humans when AI is used in real-world settings
- Consider what could go wrong when AI is applied to real-world problems

## Session Construction

The session progresses through the following sequence:

**Recap → Connect → Explore → Categorize → Analyze → Apply → Evaluate → Reflect**

### Recap

The session connects the multimodal AI concepts from the previous module to real-world applications of AI.

### Connect

Learners consider where they encounter AI in their everyday lives and how AI can support people in different situations.

### Explore

The session introduces AI applications across five industries:

- Healthcare
- Finance
- Transportation
- Entertainment
- Education

### Categorize

Learners examine how AI is used differently across these industries and identify the types of tasks AI can support.

### Analyze

Learners use the **Sense → Think → Act** model to examine real-world AI applications.

They consider:

- What information the AI receives
- What the AI does with that information
- What prediction, recommendation, or decision it produces
- What action can follow

### Apply

Learners conduct research into an AI application within an industry.

They identify:

1. The AI application
2. Who the AI helps
3. How the AI is used
4. What could go wrong
5. How the application fits the Sense → Think → Act model

### Evaluate

Learners consider the benefits and possible problems of applying AI in real-world settings.

The session emphasizes that AI can support people while humans remain involved in its use.

### Reflect

Learners reflect on how AI is already being used around them and how its role changes depending on the problem it is being used to address.

## Examples & Applications

The session explores AI applications across five industries:

### Healthcare

AI applications that can support healthcare-related tasks and processes.

### Finance

AI applications used in financial services and decision-making.

### Transportation

AI applications used in transportation and mobility.

### Entertainment

AI applications used for content, recommendations, and entertainment experiences.

### Education

AI applications that can support learning and educational activities.

The session connects these examples through the **Sense → Think → Act** model.

## Hands-On Component

**Industry AI Research Activity**

Learners choose an AI application and investigate:

1. What the AI application does
2. Who it helps
3. What information it uses
4. What the AI predicts, recommends, or produces
5. What could go wrong
6. How the application fits the Sense → Think → Act model

The activity gives learners an opportunity to investigate AI beyond familiar consumer applications.

---

## Reflection

Learners reflect on:

- Where they encounter AI in everyday life
- How AI is used across different industries
- Who benefits from an AI application
- What could go wrong
- Why humans may still need to be involved when AI is used

## Assessment & Extension

The session includes industry examples, application analysis, research, Sense → Think → Act activities, discussion, and reflection.

The extension activity asks learners to explore AI use in their own environment by asking an adult whether AI is used in their job or workplace.
