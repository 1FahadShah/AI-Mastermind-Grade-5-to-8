# Module 05 - AI Domains & Modality

> **Track A (Grades 5-8)** | **Module 5 of 8** | **4 Hours** | **Sessions 14-17**

---

## Overview

Module 05 introduces learners to different domains and modalities through which AI can process information and interact with the world.

The module moves across language, visual information, voice, and multimodal AI. Learners encounter practical examples of each domain and use AI tools to experience how different forms of information can be processed or generated.

---

## Module Purpose

The module is designed to help learners:

- Understand different AI domains and modalities
- Explore how AI works with language, images, and voice
- Recognize common applications of different AI domains
- Understand how multiple modalities can be combined
- Experiment with AI tools across different modalities
- Consider both the capabilities and risks of AI-generated content

## Session Breakdown

### Session 14 - Natural Language Processing

Introduces Natural Language Processing and explores how AI systems work with human language.

Learners examine examples such as ChatGPT, Siri, Google Translate, YouTube auto-captions, and autocomplete.

The session also introduces the simplified flow:

**Reading → Understanding → Writing Back**

The hands-on component uses QuillBot to explore paraphrasing and compare how AI transforms language.

---

### Session 15 - Computer Vision

Introduces Computer Vision and explores how AI systems work with visual information.

Learners examine applications including self-driving systems, medical imaging, social media filters, agriculture, and security.

The session introduces the simplified flow:

**Pixels / Visual Input → Detect Features → Find Patterns → Make a Prediction → Take Action**

Learners apply this flow to self-driving scenarios involving objects such as stop signs, pedestrians, and traffic lights.

---

### Session 16 - Voice AI

Introduces Voice AI and explores how AI systems process and generate spoken language.

Learners are introduced to:

- Speech-to-Text
- Text-to-Speech
- Voice generation
- Voice cloning

The hands-on activity uses ElevenLabs to create an AI-generated voice and explore text-to-speech.

The session also introduces potential misuse of voice technology, including voice scams.

---

### Session 17 - Multimodal AI

Introduces Multimodal AI and explores systems that work with more than one type of information.

Modalities explored include:

- Text
- Images
- Audio
- Video
- Code

Learners connect multimodal systems to the broader **Sense → Think → Act** model.

The hands-on component uses Leonardo AI for text-to-video generation and Gamma for presentation creation. Learners compare multimodal outputs and consider the risks associated with AI-generated media.

---

## Design Approach

The module uses a modality-by-modality progression before bringing the concepts together.

Learners first encounter AI working with **language**, then **visual information**, then **voice**, before exploring systems that combine multiple modalities.

Each session combines:

- Concept introduction
- Familiar examples
- Simplified process models
- Practical applications
- Hands-on tool exploration
- Discussion and reflection

The progression allows learners to encounter different AI modalities separately before considering how they can work together.

---

## Assessment & Reflection

Assessment across the module includes:

- Identifying AI modalities
- Recognizing real-world applications
- Applying simplified AI process flows
- Tool-based activities
- Comparing AI outputs
- Discussion
- Reflection
- Session-level knowledge checks

Learners are also encouraged to consider limitations and potential risks, particularly around generated voices and AI-generated media.

---

## Module Connection

Module 05 expands the learner's understanding of AI beyond general AI concepts and prompting by examining specific domains and modalities.

The module concludes with Multimodal AI, bringing together different forms of information before the program moves into applications of AI in everyday life and across industries.
