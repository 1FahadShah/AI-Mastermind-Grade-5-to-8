# Session 16 - Voice AI

> **Track A (Grades 5-8)** | **Module 5** | **Session 16 of 25**

---

## Overview

Session 16 introduces Voice AI and explores how AI systems can work with spoken language.

The session focuses on two key processes - Speech-to-Text (STT) and Text-to-Speech (TTS) - and examines why working with human voices can be challenging. Learners also explore voice generation and consider potential misuse of voice AI.

---

## Purpose

The session is designed to help learners:

- Understand what Voice AI means
- Understand Speech-to-Text and Text-to-Speech
- Explore how AI systems process spoken language
- Recognize why different voices can make Voice AI challenging
- Explore AI-generated voices
- Consider potential risks and misuse of voice technology

## Session Construction

The session progresses through the following sequence:

**Warm-Up → Define → Break Down → Explore → Challenge → Create → Consider → Reflect**

### Warm-Up

Learners consider how people communicate using their voices and what makes different voices distinct.

### Define

Voice AI is introduced as AI that can work with spoken language and generate speech.

### Break Down

The session introduces two important Voice AI processes:

- **Speech-to-Text (STT)** - converting spoken language into text
- **Text-to-Speech (TTS)** - converting written text into spoken language

### Explore

Learners examine examples of Voice AI and consider how these systems can recognize speech, process language, and produce spoken responses.

### Challenge

The session explores why Voice AI can be difficult.

Different voices can vary in:

- Speaking style
- Pronunciation
- Voice characteristics
- Other aspects of how people speak

The session connects these differences to the importance of data when building AI systems.

### Create

Learners use ElevenLabs to create an AI-generated voice from a description and explore text-to-speech generation.

### Consider

The session introduces the risks associated with voice cloning and AI-generated voices, including potential misuse and voice scams.

### Reflect

Learners consider how Voice AI can be useful while also thinking about how generated or cloned voices could be misused.

## Examples & Applications

The session explores Voice AI through examples of systems that can process or generate speech.

Learners consider how Voice AI can be used for:

- Speech recognition
- Spoken responses
- Voice generation
- Voice-based interaction

The session also introduces voice cloning as an example of a technology that can have both useful applications and potential risks.

---

## Hands-On Component

**AI Voice Creation Activity**

Learners use ElevenLabs to:

1. Describe a voice
2. Generate an AI voice
3. Create spoken output using text-to-speech
4. Listen to the generated result
5. Consider how the generated voice differs from human speech

The activity provides a practical introduction to AI-generated voices.

## Reflection

The hands-on activity gives learners direct experience with AI-generated speech while the discussion introduces responsible consideration of voice technology.
