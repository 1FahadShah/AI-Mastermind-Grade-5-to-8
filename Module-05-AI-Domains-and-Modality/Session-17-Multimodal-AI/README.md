# Session 17 - Multimodal AI

> **Track A (Grades 5-8)** | **Module 5** | **Session 17 of 25**

---

## Overview

Session 17 introduces Multimodal AI and explores how AI systems can work with more than one type of information.

The session brings together the modalities explored across the module and shows how AI can work with combinations of text, images, audio, video, and code.

---

## Purpose

The session is designed to help learners:

- Understand what Multimodal AI means
- Identify different types of AI modalities
- Understand how AI can work with multiple modalities
- Connect multimodal systems to the Sense → Think → Act model
- Experiment with multimodal AI tools
- Consider how combining modalities can expand what AI systems can create
- Recognize risks associated with AI-generated media

## Session Construction

The session progresses through the following sequence:

**Recap → Define → Identify → Connect → Explore → Create → Compare → Reflect**

### Recap

The session revisits the different AI modalities introduced throughout the module, including language, vision, and voice.

### Define

Multimodal AI is introduced as AI that can work with more than one type of modality.

Possible modalities include:

- Text
- Images
- Audio
- Video
- Code

### Identify

Learners identify examples of systems that combine different types of information and consider what each modality contributes.

### Connect

The session connects multimodal AI to the broader:

**Sense → Think → Act**

model.

Different forms of input can provide information that an AI system can process before producing an output or action.

### Explore

Learners explore multimodal AI tools and examine how different types of inputs and outputs can be combined.

### Create

Learners use Leonardo AI to experiment with text-to-video generation.

They create prompts, generate video, and examine the resulting output.

Learners also use Gamma to turn a topic into a presentation.

### Compare

Learners compare different multimodal outputs and consider how combining modalities changes the way information can be communicated.

The session also introduces the possibility of creating convincing but misleading AI-generated media.

### Reflect

Learners consider both the creative possibilities of multimodal AI and the risks associated with generated media.

## Examples & Applications

The session explores how AI can combine different forms of information.

Examples include:

- Text and images
- Text and video
- Audio and text
- Image and narration
- AI-generated video
- AI-generated presentations

Learners consider how different modalities can work together to communicate or create information.

---

## Hands-On Component

**Multimodal AI Creation Activity**

Learners use AI tools to create and explore multimodal outputs.

The activity includes:

1. Create a text prompt
2. Use Leonardo AI to generate a video
3. Examine the generated result
4. Use Gamma to create a presentation from a topic
5. Compare the different outputs
6. Consider how multiple modalities can be combined

The activity gives learners direct experience with AI systems that generate more than one type of content.

---

## Reflection

Learners reflect on:

- What makes an AI system multimodal
- Which modalities they encountered
- How different modalities can work together
- How multimodal AI expands creative possibilities
- How AI-generated media could be misleading or misused

The session includes modality identification, multimodal AI examples, tool exploration, video and presentation creation, comparison, discussion, and reflection.

The extension activity encourages learners to identify multimodal AI applications and explore how different modalities can be combined in a single creation.
