# Module 04 - Prompting & Human-AI Interaction

> **Track A (Grades 5-8)** | **Module 4 of 8** | **Sessions 11-13** | **3 Hours**

---

## Overview

Module 04 develops prompting as a practical skill for communicating and
working effectively with AI systems.

The module begins with the fundamentals of writing clear prompts, moves into
advanced prompting techniques, and then applies those techniques to practical
tasks.

Learners progress from understanding prompts to experimenting with different
prompt structures and using prompting for real-world tasks.

---

## Module Purpose

The module is designed to help learners:

- Understand what a prompt is
- Understand why clear instructions matter
- Write and improve prompts
- Explore advanced prompting techniques
- Experiment with different prompt structures
- Apply prompting to practical tasks
- Match tasks with appropriate AI tools
- Test and refine AI interactions
- Consider responsible use of AI when completing tasks

## Session 11 - The Art of Prompting

The first session introduces prompting as a skill for communicating with AI.

Learners compare vague and specific instructions, write prompts for different
tasks, test their prompts, and improve them based on the resulting output.

---

## Session 12 - Advanced Prompting

The second session builds on basic prompting by introducing advanced
prompting techniques.

Learners experiment with techniques such as zero-shot prompting, few-shot
prompting, role prompting, chain-of-thought prompting, and combined
prompting.

A prompt playground gives learners an opportunity to test and refine
different approaches.

---

## Session 13 - Prompting for Real Tasks

The final session applies prompting to practical situations.

Learners work with creative, everyday, research, learning, and writing tasks.
They consider which AI tool to use, create and test prompts, evaluate the
results, and apply prompting to practical situations through activities such
as the Helper Week and full-pipeline challenge.

---

## Design Approach

The materials in this module use a progression from learning the basics of
prompting to experimenting with prompting techniques and applying them to
practical tasks.

Across the three sessions, learners:

- Compare vague and specific instructions
- Write and improve prompts
- Experiment with different prompting techniques
- Combine prompting techniques
- Test prompts and observe AI responses
- Apply prompting to different types of tasks
- Consider which AI tools are appropriate
- Evaluate the usefulness of AI responses
- Reflect on responsible AI use

The module moves from **prompting fundamentals** to **prompt experimentation**
and then to **practical application**.

---

## Hands-On Work

The module includes practical prompting activities across all three sessions.

### Session 11

Learners write, test, and improve prompts for different tasks.

### Session 12

Learners use a prompt playground to experiment with advanced prompting
techniques and compare the resulting outputs.

### Session 13

Learners apply prompting to practical tasks and use the Real-Task Prompting
Playground to create, test, evaluate, and improve prompts.

---

## Assessment & Extension

Assessment and extension activities are included at the session level.

Across the module, learners engage with:

- Prompt comparisons
- Prompt-writing activities
- Prompt testing
- Prompt refinement
- Advanced prompting experiments
- Tool-selection activities
- Practical task challenges
- Response evaluation
- Discussion and reflection
- Homework and extension tasks

The activities progressively move learners from writing individual prompts
to using prompting as part of completing and evaluating practical tasks.

---

## Module Connection

Module 04 builds on the Generative AI concepts introduced in Module 03.

It develops the learner's ability to interact with AI systems before the
program moves into different AI domains and modalities in Module 05.
