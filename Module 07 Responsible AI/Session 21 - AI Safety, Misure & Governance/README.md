# Session 21 - AI Safety, Misuse & Governance

> **Track A (Grades 5-8)** | **Module 7** | **Session 21 of 25**

---

## Overview

Session 21 explores AI safety, misuse, and governance.

The session builds on the discussion of bias and fairness by examining what can happen when AI systems are used irresponsibly, misused intentionally, or deployed without appropriate safeguards.

Learners are introduced to the idea that responsible AI involves more than making AI systems capable. People also need to think about how AI is used, what risks it creates, and what rules or safeguards may be needed.

---

## Purpose

The session is designed to help learners:

- Understand what AI safety means
- Identify ways AI can be misused
- Recognize potential risks associated with AI systems
- Understand why safeguards can be necessary
- Explore the role of rules and governance in AI
- Think about responsible decisions when using AI
- Connect AI capabilities with responsibility and human oversight

## Session Construction

The session progresses through the following sequence:

**Recap → Define → Identify → Explore → Analyze → Safeguard → Govern → Reflect**

### Recap

The session revisits the ideas of bias and fairness from Session 20 and connects them to the broader question of responsible AI use.

### Define

AI safety is introduced as the consideration of how AI systems can be used safely and what can go wrong when they are used irresponsibly.

### Identify

Learners identify situations in which AI could be misused or cause harm.

Potential concerns include:

- Misleading or harmful content
- Misuse of AI-generated media
- Privacy concerns
- Overreliance on AI
- Unsafe applications of AI

### Explore

Learners examine why AI systems need to be used responsibly and why the same technology can have useful or harmful applications depending on how it is used.

### Analyze

Learners consider an AI scenario and identify:

1. What the AI system is being used for
2. What could go wrong
3. Who could be affected
4. What responsible use could look like

### Safeguard

The session introduces the idea of safeguards that can reduce risks associated with AI use.

Learners consider how people can make AI use safer through appropriate checks, boundaries, and human involvement.

### Govern

AI governance is introduced as the use of rules, policies, and decision-making processes to guide how AI is developed and used.

Learners consider why responsible AI may require more than individual users making good decisions.

### Reflect

Learners reflect on the responsibilities people have when creating, using, and making decisions about AI systems.

## Examples & Applications

The session explores situations where AI can create risks when used irresponsibly or without appropriate safeguards.

Examples can include:

- Misleading AI-generated content
- Misuse of AI-generated media
- Privacy-related concerns
- Unsafe AI applications
- Overreliance on AI-generated information

Learners consider how responsible use and appropriate safeguards can reduce potential risks.

---

## Hands-On Component

**AI Safety & Governance Scenario Activity**

Learners examine AI-related scenarios and work through a simplified responsible AI analysis.

For each scenario, learners consider:

1. What is the AI being used for?
2. What could go wrong?
3. Who could be affected?
4. What safeguard could help?
5. What human involvement is needed?
6. What rule or policy could guide responsible use?

The activity connects individual AI decisions with the broader idea of governance.

---

## Reflection

Learners reflect on:

- What makes AI use safe or unsafe
- How AI can be misused
- Why safeguards matter
- Why humans need to remain involved in important AI decisions
- How rules and policies can guide AI use
- What responsible AI use means to them

---

## Assessment & Extension

The session includes AI safety concepts, misuse scenarios, risk analysis, safeguards, governance discussions, and reflection.

The scenario activity gives learners an opportunity to identify potential risks and propose practical safeguards and governance measures.
