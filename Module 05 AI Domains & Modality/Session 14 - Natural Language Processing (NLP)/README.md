# Session 14 - Natural Language Processing

> **Track A (Grades 5-8)** | **Module 5** | **Session 14 of 25**

---

## Overview

Session 14 introduces Natural Language Processing (NLP) as a field of AI that works with human language.

The session explores how AI can process, interpret, and respond to language and introduces learners to common NLP applications they encounter in everyday life.

---

## Purpose

The session is designed to help learners:

- Understand what Natural Language Processing means
- Recognize how AI works with human language
- Explore common NLP applications
- Understand that language can have different meanings depending on context
- Explore how NLP systems process and respond to text
- Experiment with an NLP tool

## Session Construction

The session progresses through the following sequence:

**Warm-Up → Define → Explore → Model → Identify → Experiment → Compare → Reflect**

### Warm-Up

Learners explore sentences that can have different meanings depending on how they are interpreted.

Examples include ambiguous or sarcastic statements that demonstrate why understanding human language can be difficult.

### Define

Natural Language Processing is introduced as AI that works with human words and language.

### Explore

Learners examine NLP applications they may already encounter, including:

- ChatGPT
- Siri
- Google Translate
- YouTube auto-captions
- Autocomplete

### Model

The session introduces a simplified NLP flow:

**Reading → Understanding → Writing Back**

This provides a simple way to think about how language-based AI receives language, processes it, and produces a response.

### Identify

Learners identify NLP systems in everyday applications and consider what language-related task each system performs.

### Experiment

Learners use QuillBot to rewrite one of their own sentences and compare different rewriting options.

### Compare

Learners compare the original sentence with the rewritten versions and consider how the wording and meaning change.

### Reflect

Learners consider how AI handles human language and why context matters when interpreting language.

## Hands-On Component

**QuillBot Paraphrasing Activity**

Learners:

1. Write an original sentence
2. Use QuillBot to paraphrase it
3. Explore different rewriting options
4. Compare the rewritten sentence with the original
5. Examine whether the meaning changed
6. Discuss why wording and context matter

The activity gives learners a direct example of AI transforming human language.

---

## Reflection

Learners reflect on:

- How AI works with human language
- Why some sentences can have multiple meanings
- How context affects interpretation
- How paraphrasing can change wording
- Whether the meaning of a sentence stayed the same
