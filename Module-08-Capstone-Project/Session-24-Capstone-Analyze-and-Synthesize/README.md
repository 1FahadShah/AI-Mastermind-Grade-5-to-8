# Session 24 - Analyze & Synthesize

> **Track A (Grades 5-8)** | **Module 8** | **Session 24 of 25**

---

## Overview

Session 24 moves the Capstone Project from research and experimentation into analysis and synthesis.

Learners examine the evidence they have gathered, analyze their project through multiple responsible AI perspectives, and bring their findings together into a coherent research paper.

The session also develops the abstract, conclusion, future work, AI attribution, and presentation materials needed for the final delivery.

---

## Purpose

The session is designed to help learners:

- Analyze the evidence gathered during their research
- Connect research findings with their project artifact or experiment
- Examine their project through responsible AI considerations
- Synthesize findings into a clear conclusion
- Develop an abstract for the research paper
- Identify possible future work
- Document AI use and attribution
- Prepare presentation materials

## Session Construction

The session progresses through the following sequence:

**Review → Analyze → Examine → Connect → Synthesize → Conclude → Attribute → Present**

### Review

Learners review the research, evidence, experiments, and project artifact developed during Session 23.

### Analyze

Learners examine their findings and consider what the evidence shows in relation to their research question.

### Examine

The project is analyzed through five responsible AI lenses:

- Bias & Fairness
- Safety & Governance
- Ethics
- Human-in-the-Loop
- Voice Integrity

### Connect

Learners connect their research findings, practical work, and responsible AI analysis to develop a more complete understanding of their topic.

### Synthesize

Learners bring their findings together and identify the main ideas that answer their research question.

### Conclude

Learners develop:

- Conclusion
- Future work
- Abstract

These sections bring the research into a concise and structured form.

### Attribute

Learners document how AI was used during the project and include appropriate AI attribution.

This connects to the AI Diary and research integrity work from Session 23.

### Present

Learners use Gamma to begin developing presentation materials for the final capstone delivery.

## Examples & Applications

Learners analyze their own capstone project rather than working through a single predefined example.

The analysis considers five lenses:

| Lens                    | Focus                                                                                  |
| :---------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Bias & Fairness**     | How the project may affect different people or groups                                  |
| **Safety & Governance** | Potential risks, safeguards, and responsible use                                       |
| **Ethics**              | Ethical considerations surrounding the project                                         |
| **Human-in-the-Loop**   | Where people remain involved in AI-related decisions                                   |
| **Voice Integrity**     | Considerations around representation, attribution, and the use of AI-generated content |

Learners use these perspectives to strengthen their analysis and conclusions.

## Hands-On Component

**Capstone Analysis & Synthesis Activity**

Learners:

1. Review their research and evidence
2. Analyze their findings
3. Examine the project through the five responsible AI lenses
4. Connect the findings to their research question
5. Develop a conclusion
6. Identify future work
7. Write an abstract
8. Document AI use and attribution
9. Begin creating their Gamma presentation

The activity transforms the research and practical work from Session 23 into a structured final argument.

---

## Reflection

Learners reflect on:

- What their evidence shows
- How their findings answer the research question
- What they discovered through the five responsible AI lenses
- What limitations or unanswered questions remain
- How AI contributed to their project
- What they personally contributed
- What future work could build on their research

---

## Assessment & Extension

This session does not use a separate assessment quiz.

Progress is demonstrated through the development of the research paper and presentation materials, including analysis, synthesis, conclusion, future work, abstract, and AI attribution.

The completed work prepares learners for the final presentation and reflection in Session 25.
