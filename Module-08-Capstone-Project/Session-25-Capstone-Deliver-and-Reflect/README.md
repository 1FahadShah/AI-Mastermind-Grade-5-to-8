# Session 25 - Deliver & Reflect

> **Track A (Grades 5-8)** | **Module 8** | **Session 25 of 25**

---

## Overview

Session 25 concludes the Capstone Project through final delivery and reflection.

Learners present their research, finalize their research paper, document their use of AI, and reflect on what they learned and created throughout the program.

The session brings together the research, experiments, AI-assisted work, and artifacts developed across the capstone and the wider AI Mastermind program.

---

## Purpose

The session is designed to help learners:

- Deliver their completed capstone project
- Present their research and findings
- Finalize their research paper
- Explain how AI was used during the project
- Reflect on their own contributions and learning
- Review the artifacts created throughout the program
- Identify what they want to continue learning or building

## Session Construction

The session progresses through the following sequence:

**Review → Finalize → Present → Explain → Reflect → Recap → Continue**

### Review

Learners review the research, findings, presentation, research paper, and project artifacts developed throughout the capstone.

### Finalize

Learners complete the final version of their research paper and presentation.

They also review their AI attribution and ensure their project accurately represents their own work and use of AI.

### Present

Learners deliver their capstone presentation and communicate their research, findings, and conclusions.

### Explain

Learners explain their project, including:

- The research question
- What they investigated
- What they built or experimented with
- What they discovered
- How AI was used
- What they contributed

### Reflect

Learners reflect on their experience completing the research project and consider what they learned from the process.

### Recap

Learners review the major artifacts they created throughout the AI Mastermind program.

These include:

- Research Paper
- Gamma Presentation
- Prompt Library
- Lovable App
- AI Voice
- AI Video
- Teachable Machine Model
- Detective Notes + AI Diary

### Continue

The program closes with an emphasis on continuing to explore, question, and build with AI.

The closing messages include:

- **Stay Curious**
- **Stay Honest about what AI does and what you do with it**
- **Keep Building**
- **The Capstone Ends. You Don't.**

## Examples & Applications

The final session brings together the practical and research-based work completed during the program.

Learners revisit their project artifacts and consider how each demonstrates a different aspect of their AI learning.

The capstone presentation gives learners an opportunity to communicate their research and explain the relationship between their own work and AI-assisted work.

## Hands-On Component

**Capstone Presentation & Portfolio Recap**

Learners:

1. Review their completed research
2. Finalize their research paper
3. Finalize their presentation
4. Present their research and findings
5. Explain their use of AI
6. Reflect on their own contribution
7. Review their program artifacts
8. Identify what they want to continue exploring or building

The activity serves as the final delivery and reflection point of the Capstone Project.

---

## Reflection

Learners reflect on:

- What they learned during the capstone
- What they discovered through their research
- What they built or experimented with
- How AI contributed to their project
- What they contributed themselves
- Which artifact they are most proud of
- What they would improve if they continued the project
- What they want to explore next

---

## Assessment & Extension

This session does not use a separate assessment quiz.

The final capstone is demonstrated through the completed research paper, presentation, project artifacts, AI attribution, presentation delivery, and learner reflection.

The final reflection also provides an opportunity for learners to consider how they can continue learning and building beyond the program.
