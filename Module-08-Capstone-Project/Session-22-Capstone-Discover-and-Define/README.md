# Session 22 - Discover & Define

> **Track A (Grades 5-8)** | **Module 8** | **Session 22 of 25**

---

## Overview

Session 22 begins the Capstone Project by moving learners from exploring AI concepts to defining a research project of their own.

The session focuses on selecting a domain, identifying a problem, developing a research question, defining the project scope, and identifying sources that can support the research.

---

## Purpose

The session is designed to help learners:

- Begin their AI research project
- Select a domain for investigation
- Identify a problem or area of interest
- Develop a research question
- Define the scope of the project
- Identify useful sources
- Establish a clear direction for the research paper

## Session Construction

The session progresses through the following sequence:

**Discover → Choose → Identify → Question → Scope → Research → Define**

### Discover

Learners begin exploring possible areas for their capstone project.

### Choose

Learners select a domain or topic that they want to investigate.

### Identify

Learners identify a problem, question, or area within their selected domain that is worth exploring.

### Question

Learners develop a research question that will guide their project.

### Scope

Learners define what their project will and will not cover.

This helps create a manageable research direction.

### Research

Learners begin identifying sources that can support their investigation.

### Define

Learners bring their topic, problem, research question, scope, and initial sources together into a clear project direction.

## Examples & Applications

The session allows learners to choose a domain connected to AI and investigate a problem or question within that area.

Learners consider:

- What area of AI interests them
- What problem they want to explore
- What question they want their research to answer
- What information they need to investigate
- Which sources can support their research

---

## Hands-On Component

**Capstone Discovery & Definition Activity**

Learners develop the initial foundation of their research project by:

1. Selecting a domain
2. Identifying a problem or area of interest
3. Developing a research question
4. Defining the project scope
5. Identifying initial sources
6. Organizing the direction of the project

The activity establishes the starting point for the research paper developed across the remaining capstone sessions.

---

## Reflection

Learners reflect on:

- Why they selected their topic
- What problem they want to investigate
- Whether their research question is clear
- Whether their project scope is manageable
- What they still need to learn
- Which sources may help answer their question

---

## Assessment & Extension

This session does not use a separate assessment quiz.

Progress is demonstrated through the development of the capstone project foundation, including the selected domain, problem, research question, scope, and initial sources.

The resulting work becomes the starting point for the research and build phase in Session 23.
