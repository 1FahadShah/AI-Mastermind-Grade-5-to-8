# Session 23 - Research & Build

> **Track A (Grades 5-8)** | **Module 8** | **Session 23 of 25**

---

## Overview

Session 23 moves the Capstone Project from project definition into research and practical building.

Learners develop their research using relevant sources while also creating an artifact or experiment connected to their project. The session introduces the importance of documenting the research and AI-assisted work through an AI diary and maintaining integrity throughout the process.

---

## Purpose

The session is designed to help learners:

- Conduct research using relevant sources
- Gather evidence for their research question
- Build or experiment with an AI-related artifact
- Connect practical work to the research project
- Document how AI is used during the project
- Maintain integrity while using AI
- Organize research and project evidence

## Session Construction

The session progresses through the following sequence:

**Research → Gather → Build → Experiment → Document → Check → Organize**

### Research

Learners continue investigating the research question established in Session 22.

### Gather

Learners identify relevant sources and gather information and evidence that can support their research.

### Build

Learners create an artifact or practical component connected to their research project.

### Experiment

Learners test, explore, or experiment with their chosen AI-related idea or artifact.

### Document

Learners maintain an **AI Diary** to document their use of AI throughout the project.

### Check

Learners consider whether their research and practical work accurately represent what they did and what AI contributed.

### Organize

Learners organize their research, evidence, experiments, and documentation in preparation for analysis and synthesis.

## Examples & Applications

Learners connect their research to a practical AI-related artifact or experiment.

The project can involve building or exploring an AI application or other AI-related output that supports the research question.

Learners also document how AI is used during the project so that their own work and AI-assisted contributions remain distinguishable.

---

## Hands-On Component

**Capstone Research & Build Activity**

Learners:

1. Continue researching their question
2. Gather relevant sources
3. Collect evidence
4. Build or create a project artifact
5. Conduct an experiment or practical exploration
6. Record AI use in their AI Diary
7. Review their work for integrity
8. Organize evidence for the next stage

The activity connects research with practical experimentation and documentation.

---

## Reflection

Learners reflect on:

- What they learned from their research
- Which sources were useful
- What their experiment or artifact demonstrates
- How AI contributed to their work
- What they personally contributed
- Whether their research and project accurately represent their work

---

## Assessment & Extension

This session does not use a separate assessment quiz.

Progress is demonstrated through the development of the research project, including sources, evidence, the practical artifact or experiment, and AI documentation.

The completed research and build work becomes the foundation for the analysis and synthesis stage in Session 24.
