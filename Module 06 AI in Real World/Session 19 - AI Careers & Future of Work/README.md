# Session 19 - Careers & Future of Work

> **Track A (Grades 5-8)** | **Module 6** | **Session 19 of 25**

---

## Overview

Session 19 explores how AI is changing work and how people can work alongside AI.

The session moves beyond AI applications to consider the relationship between AI and different types of work. Learners explore how AI can augment human capabilities, different ways people may work with AI, and skills that remain important in an AI-enabled future.

---

## Purpose

The session is designed to help learners:

- Understand how AI can change the way people work
- Explore AI as a tool that can augment human abilities
- Recognize different ways people can work with AI
- Explore skills that are useful when working with AI
- Experiment with a no-code AI application builder
- Think about how AI may influence future careers

## Session Construction

The session progresses through the following sequence:

**Recap → Connect → Explore → Compare → Categorize → Identify → Build → Reflect**

### Recap

The session connects the real-world AI applications explored in Session 18 to the changing relationship between AI and work.

### Connect

Learners consider how AI might affect jobs they already know and how people could use AI as a tool rather than viewing AI only as a replacement for people.

### Explore

The session uses familiar roles to explore how AI can support human work.

Examples include:

- Chef
- Athlete
- Architect

Learners consider what AI could help with while people continue to contribute their own abilities and judgment.

### Compare

The session introduces the idea of **AI augmentation**, where AI supports or enhances what people can do.

The focus is on people and AI working together rather than treating AI as completely separate from human work.

### Categorize

The session introduces four job lanes:

- **Builders** - people who create and develop AI systems
- **Deciders** - people who use judgment and make decisions
- **Guardians** - people who focus on responsible and safe AI use
- **Empowered** - people who use AI to improve their own work

### Identify

Learners explore five skills that are important when working with AI:

- AI literacy
- Critical thinking
- Creativity
- Communication
- Problem solving

### Build

Learners use Lovable as a no-code application builder.

They plan an application, prompt the tool, generate an initial version, test it, and iterate.

### Reflect

Learners consider what role they might play in a future where people and AI work together.

## Hands-On Component

**No-Code AI Application Activity**

Learners use Lovable to build a simple application.

The activity follows a practical sequence:

1. Plan an application
2. Describe the idea
3. Prompt the AI tool
4. Generate an initial version
5. Test the application
6. Identify improvements
7. Iterate on the result

The activity gives learners direct experience with using AI as a tool for building rather than only consuming AI-generated content.

---

## Reflection

Learners reflect on:

- How AI can support different types of work
- The difference between replacing work and augmenting human abilities
- Which job lane interests them
- Which skills they want to develop
- How they could use AI as a tool in the future

## Assessment & Extension

The session includes career examples, AI augmentation activities, job-lane exploration, skills discussion, a no-code building activity, testing, iteration, and reflection.

The extension activity asks learners to choose a job lane and identify how they could improve their application.

Learners can also ask someone what kind of application they would want and attempt to build it.
