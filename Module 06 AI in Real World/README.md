# Module 06 - AI in Real World

> **Track A (Grades 5-8)** | **Module 6 of 8** | **2 Hours** | **Sessions 18-19**

---

## Overview

Module 06 explores how AI is used in everyday life, across industries, and in the world of work.

The module moves from examining real-world AI applications to considering how AI can support people in different careers. Learners explore practical applications, analyze how AI is used, and build a simple application using a no-code AI tool.

---

## Module Purpose

The module is designed to help learners:

- Identify AI applications in everyday life
- Explore how AI is used across different industries
- Analyze real-world AI applications
- Understand the role of humans when AI is used
- Explore how AI can augment human work
- Identify different ways people can work with AI
- Recognize skills that are useful when working with AI
- Experiment with AI as a tool for building

## Session Breakdown

### Session 18 - AI in Everyday Life & Industries

Explores how AI is used in everyday life and across five industries:

- Healthcare
- Finance
- Transportation
- Entertainment
- Education

Learners use the **Sense → Think → Act** model to analyze real-world AI applications.

The hands-on component asks learners to research an AI application, identify who it helps, examine how it works, and consider what could go wrong.

The session also introduces the idea of a **human-in-the-loop**, showing that people can remain involved when AI is used in real-world settings.

---

### Session 19 - Careers & Future of Work

Explores how AI can change the way people work and how people can work alongside AI.

Learners examine AI augmentation through familiar roles such as chefs, athletes, and architects.

The session introduces four job lanes:

- **Builders** - people who create and develop AI systems
- **Deciders** - people who use judgment and make decisions
- **Guardians** - people who focus on responsible and safe AI use
- **Empowered** - people who use AI to improve their own work

Learners also explore five skills:

- AI literacy
- Critical thinking
- Creativity
- Communication
- Problem solving

The hands-on component uses Lovable to plan, prompt, build, test, and iterate on a simple application.

---

## Design Approach

The module uses a progression from **AI applications → human involvement → work and careers**.

Session 18 focuses on where AI is being used and what it does in different real-world settings.

Session 19 extends this into the relationship between AI and human work, introducing AI augmentation, different ways people can work with AI, and skills that can support effective use of AI.

Both sessions connect AI concepts to practical situations rather than treating AI only as a technical subject.

## Hands-On Work

The module includes two practical application activities:

| Session | Activity               | Focus                                    |
| :------ | :--------------------- | :--------------------------------------- |
| **18**  | Industry AI Research   | Investigating real-world AI applications |
| **19**  | No-Code AI Application | Building, testing, and iterating with AI |

The activities move learners from analyzing existing AI applications to using AI as a tool for creating something themselves.

---

## Assessment & Reflection

Assessment and reflection across the module include:

- Real-world AI application analysis
- Industry research
- Sense → Think → Act activities
- Discussion
- Career exploration
- AI augmentation activities
- No-code building
- Testing and iteration
- Session-level knowledge checks
- Reflection on AI and human work

Learners are encouraged to consider both what AI can do and the role people continue to play when AI is applied in real-world settings.

---

## Module Connection

Module 06 shifts the program from exploring specific AI domains and modalities to examining AI in broader real-world contexts.

The module concludes by connecting AI applications to careers and future work before the program moves into questions of bias, fairness, safety, and responsible AI use.
