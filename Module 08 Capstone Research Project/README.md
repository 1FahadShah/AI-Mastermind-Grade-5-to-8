# Module 08 - Capstone Project

> **Track A (Grades 5-8)** | **Module 8 of 8** | **4 Hours** | **Sessions 22-25**

---

## Overview

Module 08 is the capstone of the AI Mastermind program.

The module takes learners through a research paper project from initial topic discovery and research question development to research, practical experimentation, analysis, presentation, and reflection.

Learners combine concepts from the earlier modules with their own research and project work while documenting how AI is used throughout the process.

---

## Module Purpose

The module is designed to help learners:

- Develop an AI-related research project
- Select a domain and identify a problem
- Formulate a research question
- Define project scope
- Conduct research using relevant sources
- Build or experiment with an AI-related artifact
- Analyze and synthesize research findings
- Examine their project through responsible AI considerations
- Develop a research paper and presentation
- Document AI use and attribution
- Present their work
- Reflect on their learning and project experience

---

---

## Session Breakdown

### Session 22 - Discover & Define

Begins the capstone by establishing a clear research direction.

Learners:

- Select a domain
- Identify a problem or area of interest
- Develop a research question
- Define the project scope
- Identify initial sources

The session establishes the foundation for the research paper and practical project work.

---

### Session 23 - Research & Build

Moves the project from definition into research and practical experimentation.

Learners:

- Continue researching their question
- Gather sources and evidence
- Build or create a project artifact
- Conduct an experiment or practical exploration
- Document AI use through an AI Diary
- Review their work for integrity
- Organize evidence for analysis

The session connects research with practical AI-related work and documentation.

---

### Session 24 - Analyze & Synthesize

Moves the project from research and experimentation into analysis and synthesis.

Learners examine their findings through five responsible AI lenses:

- Bias & Fairness
- Safety & Governance
- Ethics
- Human-in-the-Loop
- Voice Integrity

Learners then develop their conclusion, future work, abstract, AI attribution, and presentation materials.

---

### Session 25 - Deliver & Reflect

Concludes the capstone through final presentation and reflection.

Learners:

- Finalize their research paper
- Finalize their presentation
- Present their research and findings
- Explain their use of AI
- Review their own contributions
- Reflect on the capstone experience
- Recap the artifacts created throughout the program
- Consider what they want to continue exploring

The program closes with:

**Stay Curious**

**Stay Honest about what AI does and what you do with it**

**Keep Building**

**The Capstone Ends. You Don't.**

---

## Design Approach

The module uses a progression from **definition → research and building → analysis and synthesis → delivery and reflection**.

Each session advances the same capstone project rather than functioning as an isolated lesson.

The learner's work develops across the four sessions:

**Research Direction**

↓

**Evidence + Artifact**

↓

**Analysis + Synthesis**

↓

**Research Paper + Presentation + Reflection**

This structure allows learners to carry their research and project work forward from one session to the next.

---

## Hands-On Work

The capstone combines research with practical AI-related project work.

Learners may produce and review artifacts connected to their project while maintaining documentation of their AI use.

The broader program artifact recap includes:

- Research Paper
- Gamma Presentation
- Prompt Library
- Lovable App
- AI Voice
- AI Video
- Teachable Machine Model
- Detective Notes + AI Diary

---

## Responsible AI Integration

Responsible AI considerations continue into the capstone rather than remaining limited to Module 07.

During the analysis stage, learners examine their project through:

- Bias & Fairness
- Safety & Governance
- Ethics
- Human-in-the-Loop
- Voice Integrity

Learners also document their AI use and attribution as part of the research process.

---

## Assessment & Reflection

The capstone sessions do not use separate assessment quizzes.

Progress is demonstrated through the development and completion of the research project.

Evidence of progress includes:

- Defined research question
- Project scope
- Research sources
- Research evidence
- Practical artifact or experiment
- AI Diary
- Analysis
- Synthesis
- Abstract
- Conclusion
- Future work
- AI attribution
- Research paper
- Presentation
- Final reflection

---

## Final Capstone Outputs

By the end of the module, learners have developed a research-based project that includes a completed research paper and presentation.

The capstone also brings together the practical artifacts and documentation developed throughout the program.

The final project demonstrates the learner's ability to research an AI-related topic, build or experiment with AI, analyze the results, consider responsible AI issues, communicate findings, and reflect on their own work.

---

## Module Connection

Module 08 serves as the final module of the AI Mastermind program.

It brings together concepts and practical work from the earlier modules and gives learners an opportunity to apply them within an independent research project.

The module concludes with the delivery and reflection of the completed capstone.
