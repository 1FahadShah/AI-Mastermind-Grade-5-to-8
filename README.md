# AI Mastermind Program

> **Track A (Grades 5-8)** | **25 Sessions** | **8 Modules** | **25 Hours** | **1:1 Online AI Mentoring**

---

## Overview

AI Mastermind is a 25-session AI learning program designed for students in Grades 5-8.

The program introduces learners to the foundations of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Generative AI, Prompting, AI Domains, Real-World AI, Responsible AI, and a final research-based Capstone Project.

The program combines conceptual learning with hands-on exploration of AI tools. Learners move from understanding how AI works to using AI, analyzing its applications and limitations, and developing their own research project.

---

## Program Goals

The program is designed to help learners:

- Understand foundational AI concepts
- Explore how machines learn from data
- Understand Generative AI and Large Language Models
- Develop practical prompting skills
- Explore language, vision, voice, and multimodal AI
- Understand how AI is used in everyday life and industries
- Explore AI's relationship with careers and future work
- Recognize issues involving bias, fairness, safety, misuse, and governance
- Conduct an AI-related research project
- Build or experiment with AI-related artifacts
- Communicate research findings through a research paper and presentation
- Reflect on how AI was used throughout their work

## Module Breakdown

### Module 01 - Foundations of Artificial Intelligence

**Sessions 01-02**

Introduces the fundamental ideas behind Artificial Intelligence.

Learners explore:

- What AI is
- Where AI appears in everyday life
- Sense → Think → Act
- AI vs humans
- Common AI myths
- Narrow AI vs General AI
- Data
- Algorithms
- Training
- Models
- Predictions

---

### Module 02 - How Machines Learn?

**Sessions 03-06**

Introduces Machine Learning and how AI systems learn patterns from data.

Learners explore:

- Machine Learning
- Supervised Learning
- Unsupervised Learning
- Reinforcement Learning
- Datasets
- Features
- Training and testing
- Generalization
- Overfitting
- Bias in data
- Training AI models

Hands-on activities include Teachable Machine and dataset-based experiments.

---

### Module 03 - Generative AI & Modern Models

**Sessions 07-10**

Introduces Generative AI and modern AI models.

Learners explore:

- Generative AI
- Large Language Models
- Next-word prediction
- AI capabilities
- AI limitations
- Hallucinations
- Bias
- Context limitations
- Fact-checking AI outputs

Learners also experiment with generative AI tools and examine where AI systems can be useful and where their outputs need to be checked.

---

### Module 04 - Prompting & Human-AI Interaction

**Sessions 11-13**

Develops practical prompting skills and applies them to real tasks.

Learners explore:

- Prompting fundamentals
- Prompting techniques
- Advanced prompting
- Tool selection
- Prompt testing
- Prompt improvement
- Creative tasks
- Everyday tasks
- Research tasks
- Learning tasks
- Writing tasks
- Responsible AI use

The module progresses from learning prompting techniques to applying them to practical situations.

---

### Module 05 - AI Domains & Modality

**Sessions 14-17**

Introduces different domains and modalities through which AI can process and generate information.

Learners explore:

- Natural Language Processing
- Computer Vision
- Voice AI
- Multimodal AI
- Text
- Images
- Audio
- Video
- Code

Hands-on exploration includes language transformation, visual AI scenarios, AI-generated voices, video generation, and AI-generated presentations.

---

### Module 06 - AI in Real World

**Sessions 18-19**

Explores how AI is used in everyday life, across industries, and in the world of work.

Learners explore:

- AI applications
- Healthcare
- Finance
- Transportation
- Entertainment
- Education
- Human-in-the-loop
- AI augmentation
- AI and careers
- Future of work
- AI literacy
- Critical thinking
- Creativity
- Communication
- Problem solving

Learners also use a no-code AI application builder to create and iterate on an application.

---

### Module 07 - Responsible AI

**Sessions 20-21**

Introduces responsible AI through bias, fairness, safety, misuse, and governance.

Learners explore:

- AI bias
- Fairness
- Data bias
- Algorithmic bias
- Human bias
- AI auditing
- AI safety
- AI misuse
- Safeguards
- Human oversight
- Rules and policies
- AI governance
- Accountability

Learners analyze real-world cases and AI-related scenarios to identify risks and consider possible improvements and safeguards.

---

### Module 08 - Capstone Project

**Sessions 22-25**

The final module is a research-paper-based Capstone Project.

Learners progress through:

**Discover & Define**

↓

**Research & Build**

↓

**Analyze & Synthesize**

↓

**Deliver & Reflect**

The capstone includes:

- Domain selection
- Problem identification
- Research question
- Project scope
- Source gathering
- Research evidence
- Practical AI-related artifact or experiment
- AI Diary
- Analysis
- Synthesis
- Responsible AI analysis
- Conclusion
- Future work
- Abstract
- AI attribution
- Research paper
- Presentation
- Final reflection

## Learning Experience

Each instructional session is supported by a consistent set of learning materials.

### Teaching Deck

The instructor-led presentation used during the session.

### Study Material

The learner-facing reference containing the concepts, examples, explanations, and activities covered in the session.

### Assessment Quiz

A session-level knowledge check used across Sessions 01-21.

### Capstone Work

Sessions 22-25 are research-paper-based and therefore do not use separate assessment quizzes. Progress is demonstrated through the ongoing development of the capstone project and its final outputs.

## Capstone & Research

The final four sessions form a continuous research project rather than four independent lessons.

Learners begin by defining a research direction, then gather evidence and build or experiment with an AI-related artifact.

They analyze their findings through responsible AI considerations before producing their final research paper and presentation.

The capstone also requires learners to document their use of AI and distinguish their own contributions from AI-assisted work.

---

## Responsible AI Throughout the Program

Responsible AI is introduced explicitly in Module 07 and continues into the Capstone Project.

Learners encounter questions around:

- Bias
- Fairness
- Safety
- Misuse
- Governance
- Ethics
- Human involvement
- AI attribution
- Research integrity

The capstone applies these considerations through five analysis lenses:

- Bias & Fairness
- Safety & Governance
- Ethics
- Human-in-the-Loop
- Voice Integrity
