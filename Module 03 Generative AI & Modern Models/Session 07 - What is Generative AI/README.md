# Session 07 - Generative AI

> **Track A (Grades 5-8)** | **Module 3** | **Session 7 of 25**

---

## Overview

Session 07 introduces Generative AI as AI that can create new content.

The session builds on the Machine Learning concepts from Module 02 and
introduces learners to generative AI through practical examples and a
hands-on creative activity.

---

## Purpose

The session is designed to help learners:

- Understand what Generative AI is
- Recognize different types of content Generative AI can create
- Identify common Generative AI applications
- Understand the role of instructions when working with Generative AI
- Experience AI-assisted content creation
- Recognize the learner's role in directing the AI

## Session Construction

The session progresses through the following sequence:

**Recap → Introduce → Explore → Create → Compare → Reflect → Extend**

### Recap

The session connects back to the Machine Learning concepts covered in
Module 02.

### Introduce

Generative AI is introduced as AI that creates content rather than only
classifying or predicting information.

Examples include generated text, images, music, and video.

### Explore

Learners examine examples of content created with Generative AI and consider
what makes these systems different from the Machine Learning applications
explored earlier.

### Create

Learners use a Generative AI tool to create a birthday-themed artifact.

They provide instructions to the AI and observe how the generated result
changes based on what they ask for.

### Compare

Learners compare different generated results and consider how the quality and
specificity of their instructions can affect what the AI produces.

### Reflect

Learners consider the roles of the AI and the human in the creative process,
including what the AI contributed and what they directed.

### Extend

The session prepares learners for Session 08, which moves from Generative AI
in general to Large Language Models and how they work with language.

## Examples & Explanations

The session introduces Generative AI through examples of AI-created content.

The birthday-themed activity provides a concrete example of using Generative
AI to create an artifact from a learner's instructions.

The session also emphasizes the learner's role in directing the creative
process.

---

## Hands-On Component

Learners use a Generative AI tool to create a birthday-themed artifact.

The activity involves:

1. Providing an idea or instruction
2. Generating content with AI
3. Reviewing the result
4. Guiding the AI toward the desired outcome

The activity gives learners direct experience with AI-assisted creation.

---

## Reflection

Learners reflect on:

- What the AI created
- What they contributed
- How their instructions affected the result
- Their role in directing the AI

The session frames AI as a creative partner while keeping the learner in the
role of director.

---

## Assessment & Extension

The session includes discussion, exploration of Generative AI examples,
hands-on content creation, and reflection on the human-AI creative process.
