# Module 03 - Generative AI & Modern Models

> **Track A (Grades 5-8)** | **Module 3 of 8** | **Sessions 7-10** | **4 Hours**

---

## Overview

Module 03 introduces learners to Generative AI and modern language-based AI
systems.

The module begins with Generative AI and the creation of new content, then
moves into Large Language Models, AI hallucinations, and the strengths and
limitations of Generative AI.

The module also gives learners opportunities to interact directly with
Generative AI and examine how humans should work with and evaluate AI output.

---

## Module Purpose

The module is designed to help learners:

- Understand what Generative AI is
- Recognize different types of content Generative AI can create
- Understand the basic idea behind Large Language Models
- Understand next-word prediction
- Recognize common LLM capabilities and limitations
- Understand AI hallucinations
- Distinguish confidence from accuracy
- Identify why AI-generated information can be incorrect
- Understand common strengths and limitations of Generative AI
- Consider when AI should and should not be used
- Understand the role of human review and responsibility when using AI

## Session 07 - Generative AI

The first session introduces Generative AI as AI that can create new content.

Learners explore different forms of generated content and use an AI tool to
create a birthday-themed artifact.

The activity introduces the learner's role in directing AI-generated content.

---

## Session 08 - Large Language Models

The second session introduces Large Language Models and the idea of
next-word prediction.

Learners explore common LLM capabilities such as answering questions,
summarizing, translating, brainstorming, and writing.

The session also introduces limitations including hallucinations, outdated
information, bias, context limitations, and lack of true understanding.

---

## Session 09 - AI Hallucinations

The third session focuses on situations where AI generates incorrect,
fabricated, or nonsensical information while presenting it confidently.

Learners examine confidence vs. accuracy, possible causes of hallucinations,
warning signs, and ways to verify AI-generated information.

The session includes a comparison activity using ChatGPT and NotebookLM.

---

## Session 10 - Strengths & Limits of Generative AI

The final session brings together the previous sessions by examining what
Generative AI does well and where it has limitations.

Learners analyze an AI tool, consider appropriate and inappropriate uses,
and reflect on the role of humans in reviewing and using AI output.

---

## Design Approach

The materials in this module use a progression from understanding Generative
AI to examining how language models work and then evaluating their reliability
and practical use.

Across the four sessions, learners:

- Explore Generative AI through practical examples
- Create content with AI
- Examine how language models generate responses
- Compare AI capabilities with human abilities
- Investigate AI hallucinations
- Practice checking AI-generated information
- Analyze AI strengths and limitations
- Consider appropriate and inappropriate AI use
- Reflect on the role of humans when working with AI

The module moves from **creating with AI** to **understanding and evaluating
AI output**.

---

## Hands-On Work

The module includes direct interaction with Generative AI tools.

### Session 07

Learners use a Generative AI tool to create a birthday-themed artifact and
guide the generated result.

### Session 08

Learners explore Large Language Models and their responses through
interactive examples.

### Session 09

Learners compare AI-generated answers with information from a reliable source
using ChatGPT and NotebookLM.

### Session 10

Learners conduct a Generative AI analysis covering a tool's strengths,
limitations, prompts, hallucination examples, and appropriate uses.

---

## Assessment & Extension

Assessment and extension activities are included at the session level.

Across the module, learners engage with:

- Recap questions
- Concept exploration
- AI-generated content activities
- Discussion
- Comparison activities
- Hallucination analysis
- Source verification
- Tool analysis
- Reflection
- Homework and extension tasks

The activities progressively shift from using Generative AI to critically
examining its outputs and limitations.

---

## Module Connection

Module 03 builds on the Machine Learning concepts introduced in Module 02.

It provides the foundation for the next stage of the program, where learners
move into prompting and human-AI interaction.
