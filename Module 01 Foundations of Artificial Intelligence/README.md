# Module 01 - Foundations of AI

> **Track A (Grades 5-8)** | **Module 1 of 8** | **Sessions 1-2** | **2 Hours**

---

## Overview

Module 01 introduces learners to the foundations of Artificial Intelligence.

The module begins with what AI is, where learners encounter it in everyday life,
and how AI differs from humans. It then introduces the core terminology used
to describe how AI systems work.

The module establishes the concepts needed for the transition into Machine
Learning in the next module.

---

## Module Purpose

The module is designed to help learners:

- Understand what Artificial Intelligence means
- Recognize AI systems in everyday life
- Understand the **Sense → Think → Act** model
- Distinguish AI capabilities from human abilities
- Identify common misconceptions about AI
- Understand AI as a tool
- Understand the roles of data, algorithms, training, models, and predictions
- Connect these concepts into a simple AI process

## Session 01 - Introduction to AI

The first session establishes a basic understanding of AI through familiar
examples, the **Sense → Think → Act** model, AI myths, Narrow AI vs. General
AI, and the idea of AI as a tool.

Learners also interact with an AI storytelling tool and reflect on the role
of humans when working with AI.

---

## Session 02 - AI Concepts & Terminology

The second session introduces the core terminology used to describe AI systems:

**Data → Algorithm → Training → Model → Prediction**

Learners connect these concepts using examples of everyday AI systems and
explore them through Google Gemini.

---

## Design Approach

The materials in this module use a combination of:

- Familiar real-world examples
- Simple explanations and analogies
- Conceptual models
- Discussion and reflection
- Hands-on interaction with AI tools
- Practical extension tasks
- Connections between sessions

The module moves from introducing AI as a concept to giving learners
terminology they can use to describe AI systems.

---

## Hands-On Work

The module includes direct interaction with AI tools.

### Session 01

**Google Storybook**

Learners create and guide an AI-generated story.

### Session 02

**Google Gemini**

Learners explore how an AI system responds to prompts and discuss ideas
around training, prediction, and whether AI is actually thinking.

---

## Assessment & Extension

Assessment and extension activities are included at the session level.

Across the module, learners engage with:

- In-session questions
- Discussion
- Reflection
- Hands-on activities
- Session-level assessment
- Homework and extension tasks

The Session 01 homework involves creating an AI story.

The Session 02 homework involves analyzing an AI system using the concepts
of data, algorithm, training, model, and prediction.

---

## Module Connection

Module 01 provides the foundation for the next stage of the program.

The following module builds on the terminology introduced here and moves into
Machine Learning, different types of learning, data, and model training.
