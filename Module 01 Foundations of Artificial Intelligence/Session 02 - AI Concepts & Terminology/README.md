# Session 02 - AI Concepts & Terminology

> **Track A (Grades 5-8)** | **Module 1** | **Session 2 of 25**

---

## Overview

Session 02 introduces the core terms used to describe how AI systems work.

The session moves from the general idea of AI introduced in Session 01 to the
building blocks behind AI systems: **Data, Algorithm, Training, Model, and
Prediction**.

These concepts are brought together through the **AI Recipe**:

**Data + Algorithm → Training → Model → Prediction**

---

## Purpose

The session is designed to help learners:

- Explain what data is
- Explain what an algorithm is
- Explain what training means for an AI system
- Explain what a model is
- Explain what a prediction is
- Connect these concepts into one AI process

## Session Construction

The session progresses through the following sequence:

**Recap → Introduce → Break Down → Apply → Explore → Reflect → Extend**

### Recap

The session begins by revisiting the previous session through the learner's
AI Storybook activity and questions about AI, **Sense → Think → Act**, and the
idea that AI is a tool.

### Introduce

The session introduces the terminology needed to talk about AI systems and
briefly places **AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI** in
relation to one another.

### Break Down

The core terms are introduced individually:

- **Data** - the information AI learns from
- **Algorithm** - the step-by-step instructions AI follows
- **Training** - the process of learning from data
- **Model** - the trained AI ready to use
- **Prediction** - the AI's best guess about something new

### Apply

Learners work through examples and activities that ask them to identify
different parts of the AI process.

### Explore

Learners use Google Gemini to observe the concepts in action by asking about
training, changing the audience for an explanation, and having Gemini
continue a story.

### Reflect

Learners discuss whether Gemini was actually thinking, what surprised them,
and whether it produced anything incorrect.

### Extend

An **AI Detective** homework task asks learners to choose an AI system they
use regularly and analyze its data, algorithm, training, model, and
predictions.

## Examples & Explanations

The session uses familiar analogies and everyday examples to explain the
core terminology.

Data is compared to fuel or ingredients, algorithms to recipes or
step-by-step instructions, training to practice, and models to the trained
result.

Everyday systems such as Netflix, Google Maps, Spotify, Amazon, and YouTube
are used to show how AI makes predictions.

---

## Hands-On Component

**Tool:** Google Gemini

Learners explore the AI Recipe by:

1. Asking Gemini what it was trained on
2. Asking it to explain the same topic for different audiences
3. Asking it to continue a story
4. Observing the resulting responses
5. Discussing what the activity reveals about models, training, and
   prediction

The activity connects the terminology introduced in the session to an AI
system learners can interact with directly.

---

## Reflection

Learners discuss:

- Whether Gemini was really thinking
- What surprised them about its responses
- Whether Gemini produced anything that was incorrect
- The difference between making predictions and thinking

---

## Assessment & Extension

The session includes recap questions, terminology activities, discussion,
hands-on exploration, and an **AI Detective** homework task.

For homework, learners select one AI system such as YouTube, Netflix, Spotify,
Google Maps, Snapchat Filters, ChatGPT, or Gemini and answer five questions
about its data, algorithm, training, model, and predictions.
