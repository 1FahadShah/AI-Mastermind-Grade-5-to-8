# Session 01 - Introduction to AI

> **Track A (Grades 5-8)** | **Module 1** | **Session 1 of 25**

---

## Overview

Session 01 introduces learners to Artificial Intelligence through familiar examples and a simple model for understanding how AI systems interact with the world.

The session establishes the foundation for the program before learners move into algorithms, data, models, training, and prediction.

---

## Purpose

The session is designed to help learners:

- Understand what AI means
- Recognize AI in everyday life
- Understand a simple model of how AI systems work
- Distinguish AI capabilities from human abilities
- Question common assumptions about AI
- Understand AI as a tool

## Session Construction

The session progresses through the following sequence:

**Connect → Explain → Illustrate → Explore → Reflect → Extend**

### Connect

The session begins with questions about learners' existing experiences with smart devices and AI.

### Explain

AI is introduced through a simple definition and the **Sense → Think → Act** model.

### Illustrate

Everyday AI examples are used to make the concept familiar. The session also addresses common AI misconceptions, including the idea that AI is only robots, is always correct, or has feelings.

### Explore

Learners interact with an AI storytelling tool and guide the AI through the creation of a story.

### Reflect

Learners consider what the AI contributed, what they contributed, and their role in directing the result.

### Extend

A story-creation task continues the learning outside the session. The next session then introduces algorithms, data, training, models, and prediction.

## Core Concepts

- Artificial Intelligence
- Everyday AI

---

## Examples & Explanations

The session uses familiar AI applications and simple comparisons to introduce abstract concepts.

The **Sense → Think → Act** model provides a simple way to describe what an AI system receives, processes, and produces.

---

## Hands-On Component

**Tool:** Google Storybook

Learners create a story together with AI by:

1. Providing an opening idea
2. Observing the AI generate the next part
3. Guiding what happens next
4. Continuing the story

The activity gives learners an opportunity to experience AI assistance while remaining involved in directing the outcome.

---

## Reflection

Learners reflect on:

- What the AI needed from them
- How the AI helped
- Their role in creating the final story

---

## Assessment & Extension

The session includes discussion, reflection, a practical AI activity, and a homework task in which learners create their own AI story and save or share it.
